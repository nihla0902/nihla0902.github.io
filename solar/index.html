1 kW rooftop design with obstructions (practice design)

Panel placement around trees and a tank, with a payback estimate.

The problem

On a roof with trees and a tank, panels placed in the wrong spot lose output to shade. The job is to find where three panels fit with the least shading, then check that the system still pays back.

What I did

  • Modelled the roof and its obstructions in OpenSolar.
  • Placed three 335 W panels (1.005 kW) in the least shaded area, facing 213° at a 20° tilt.
  • Chose the inverter, then changed it from 2 kW to 1.5 kW to fit the 1 kW array better.
  • Produced a full proposal with energy, savings and payment pages.

How it works

Shading loss came out at 4.3%. The estimated output is 1,368 kWh a year, which is 127% of the household's modelled use of about 1,079 kWh a year.

With a Kerala domestic tariff in OpenSolar, the first-year bill saving is ₹3,782 and the payback is 11 years on the tool's price of ₹50,250.

Result

A finished proposal for a roof with several obstructions. Prices and tariffs come from OpenSolar's settings, so the money figures are estimates.

What I would improve

Next: compare other panel positions and tilts, and check the inverter choice against the array more closely.

8.71 kW rooftop design for a commercial building (practice design)

A 26-panel commercial array with tariff-based savings.

My role
Designed the system and prepared the proposal
Team
Individual practice project
When
2026
Tools
OpenSolar
Status
Practice design, not a client quote

The problem

Commercial users pay a different tariff from homes, and their roofs are usually larger and simpler. This design practises the straightforward case: an unshaded roof, sized to the building's use.

What I did

  • Placed 26 panels of 335 W (8.71 kW) at a 20° tilt facing 188°, with no shading loss.
  • Set the bills to be calculated with a Kerala commercial tariff (KSEB LT-VII(A)).
  • Produced a full proposal with energy, savings and payment pages.

How it works

The tool estimates 12,508 kWh a year, which is 125% of the 10,000 kWh a year used in the model.

First-year bill savings are ₹71,672, and the payback is 5 years 7 months on the tool's price of ₹435,500.

Result

A finished commercial proposal. Prices and tariffs come from OpenSolar's settings, so the money figures are estimates.

What I would improve

This proposal lists the panels but no inverter. The next version needs an inverter and a string design.
